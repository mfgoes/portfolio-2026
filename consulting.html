03Process

How working together goes

  1. 1

    A 30-minute call

    You show me the product and the numbers you have. I tell you honestly whether I can help, and which package fits.

  2. 2

    We start with one flow

    Either as the audit or as your first Friday. I walk every screen as a new customer would and check it against your analytics, so the first fixes are ones you can measure.

  3. 3

    Fridays, and async in between

    I do the deep work on Fridays. During the week you reach me in Slack or Figma comments, and I usually answer within a day. No standing meetings unless you want them.
